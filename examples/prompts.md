# Example prompts

Ask in Chinese or English; your assistant calls the tools (which take
Traditional Chinese) for you. Asking it to cite articles and decisions keeps
answers grounded.

## Laws

- 民法第 184 條的內容是什麼？
- What does Article 184 of Taiwan's Civil Code say? Give me the official English translation too.
- 勞動基準法關於資遣費的規定有哪些？請列出條號。
- 公司法的章節架構是什麼？
- 個人資料保護法第 20 條目前的條文？有沒有尚未生效的修正？

## Court decisions

- 最高法院對「借名登記」契約的效力有什麼見解？請引用判決。
- Find Supreme Court decisions on drunk-driving hit-and-run cases and summarise the reasoning.
- 列出所有含「契約承擔」的最高法院判決，共有幾筆？
- 近 5 年含「酒駕致死」的判決有幾件？
- 大法庭對「未遂犯與既遂犯的區分標準」作過什麼裁定？

## Combining

- 車禍對方逃逸，我可以主張哪些權利？請引用相關法條與最高法院判決。
- Explain the elements of fraud under Taiwan's Criminal Code (刑法 339), with one Supreme Court decision applying it.

Results are research material, not legal advice.
